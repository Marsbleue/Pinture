## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an uncompromising editorial aesthetic inspired by heritage art supply houses, high-end museum publications, and master artist studios. The interface balances tactile artisanal warmth with disciplined, minimalist luxury. It speaks directly to seasoned fine artists, oil painters, classical illustrators, and discerning collectors who regard their tools as instruments of precision and heritage.

The visual style blends **Minimalism** with **Tactile Editorial Craft**:
- **Generous Canvas:** Ample breathing room evoking unprimed Belgian linen and cold-press watercolor sheets.
- **Micro-Deliberation:** Whispering hairline rules, exacting typographic grids, and subtle material warmth over loud digital gimmicks.
- **Understated Gravitas:** Restrained contrast, deep pigment-rich tones, and quiet metallic accents that feel hand-applied rather than synthetic.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in classical studio pigments, raw paper pulp, and atelier metals:

- **Primary (`#1A1817` - Bone Charcoal):** An uncompromising deep espresso-charcoal holding all principal text, structural framing, and authoritative actions. Replaces synthetic `#000000` with natural pigment depth.
- **Secondary (`#C06040` - Burnt Sienna):** A vibrant, earthy terracotta drawn from natural iron oxides. Used for focal callouts, selected states, curated studio badges, and interactive emphasis.
- **Tertiary (`#D4AF37` - Muted Leaf Gold):** A quiet, patinated brass used sparingly for gilded hallmarks, limited batch editions, and subtle heritage stamps.
- **Neutral Surface Tier (`#FAF8F5` Raw Parchment & `#F3EFEA` Creamy Canvas):** Warm, calming paper grounds that eliminate clinical cold-white eye fatigue.
- **Border & Rule Tone (`#E6E0D6` Linen Thread):** Low-contrast dividers echoing delicate bookbinding thread and plate impressions.

## Typography

The typography couples classical renaissance proportions with modern catalog clarity:

- **EB Garamond (Editorial Voice):** Used across display headers, editorial quotes, and collection titles. Natural ligatures and humanistic stroke weights evoke letterpress literature. Italic cuts are deployed selectively for medium indicators (e.g., *Series VII Kolinsky Red Sable* or *En Plein Air*).
- **DM Sans (Technical Engine):** A geometric, calm sans-serif engineered for effortless scanning across pigment codes (e.g., *PR108*, *Lightfastness ASTM I*), pricing structures, catalog specs, and commerce navigation.
- **Labels & Metas:** Always set with uppercase tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.08em` to `0.1em`) to replicate engraved specimen tags and archival catalog labels.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a disciplined **12-column editorial grid** on desktop paired with expansive structural margins:

- **Rhythm & Structure:** Desktop viewports utilize generous margins (`3.5rem`) to create an art gallery matting effect around products and stories. Outer sections breathe through `space-xl` and higher multiples, enforcing intentional pacing over dense catalog walls.
- **Adaptive Breakpoints:**
  - **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns with `1.5rem` gutters. Asymmetric pairings (e.g., 5-column editorial manifesto against 7-column specimen photo).
  - **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, `1.25rem` gutters, and `2rem` outer margins.
  - **Mobile (<768px):** 4 columns, `1rem` gutters, and `1.25rem` canvas margins. Product listings collapse into clean 2-column or single full-bleed parchment cards.
- **Section Breaks:** Whitespace takes precedence over heavy divider lines. When separation is necessary, use single-pixel rules in `#E6E0D6`.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy in this system avoids loud synthetic drop shadows in favor of **Tonal Layers** and **Low-Contrast Outlines**:

- **Layered Paper Grounds:** Depth is constructed by resting `#FAF8F5` surface containers over subtle `#F3EFEA` foundational sections, mimicking overlaid sheets of hand-torn paper.
- **Low-Contrast Ghost Outlines:** Cards, spec panels, and input boundaries employ hairline outlines (`1px solid #E6E0D6`).
- **Whispering Ambient Shadows:** High-order floating elements (such as cart drawers, quick-view flyouts, and sticky navigation headers) rely on an imperceptible, warm-pigmented ambient diffusion:
  - `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(26, 24, 23, 0.05), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(26, 24, 23, 0.03)`
- **Embossed States:** Clickable specimens evoke letterpress debossing upon `:active` state with faint inset boundary shifts rather than harsh motion blurs.

## Shapes

The design system embraces an uncompromising **Sharp (`0`)** shape language:

- **Archival Precision:** Edges remain crisp and unrounded (`0px` border-radius), honoring guillotine-cut paper blocks, hand-stretched linen panels, and raw-cut wooden pencil casings.
- **Tailored Exceptions:** Only pure circular geometry is permitted for circular artist pigment swatches, brush diameter indicators, and discrete circular badge counter tags.
- **Discipline:** Rectangles must remain strictly orthogonal. This reinforces the architectural and bookbinding pedigree of fine art craft.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#1A1817`, text `#FAF8F5`, 0px border radius, uppercase `label-md` tracking. Padding: `1rem 2rem`. Subtle hover transition to `#C06040` (Burnt Sienna).
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background transparent, border `1px solid #1A1817`, text `#1A1817`. On hover, fills with `#F3EFEA`.
- **Text Link:** EB Garamond Italic or DM Sans with an underline offset of `4px` in `#D4AF37` (Muted Brass).

### Chips & Badges
- **Specimen Badges:** Background `#F3EFEA`, border `1px solid #E6E0D6`, text `#1A1817`, `label-sm` casing.
- **Heritage & Limited Hallmarks:** Delicate `#C06040` terracotta text with a `1px` burnt sienna left border, functioning as a fine collector seal.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Input Fields:** Flat `#FAF8F5` or transparent background with a single bottom rule (`1px solid #1A1817`) or full hairline border (`1px solid #E6E0D6`). Text set in `body-md`. Focus transitions border to `#1A1817` with no glowing halo.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Sharp square indicators (`0px` radius) with `1px solid #1A1817`. Checked state filled with solid `#1A1817` or `#C06040` featuring an inner hairline inset.

### Cards
- **Product & Specimen Cards:** Sharp boundary, unadorned `#FAF8F5` canvas with a fine `1px solid #E6E0D6` perimeter. Product imagery should appear against natural studio light or pale neutral stone with zero harsh drop shadows.
- **Editorial Cards:** Full-bleed natural imagery complemented by an overlaid or adjacent `#FAF8F5` typographic parchment plate displaying EB Garamond headlines and quiet DM Sans author attribution.

### Lists & Technical Tables
- **Pigment & Material Specifications:** Table rows separated by hairline `#E6E0D6` borders. Monospaced or tracked sans labels for pigment chemistry codes, lightfastness ratings, opacity indexes, and grind grain sizes.
- **Studio Notes Accordion:** Elegant accordion headers using EB Garamond with delicate `+` and `−` symbols rendered with hairline strokes.
